# Community conduct

Be respectful, patient and specific. Welcome people with different levels of experience. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks, harassment or discrimination.

Do not post private information, credentials, unsolicited advertising or harmful content. Obtain permission before sharing another person's material.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary. For a conduct concern, contact the repository owner through an available GitHub contact channel without publishing the affected person's private information. No response-time guarantee is offered.
